How it works

Four agents, working together

Each step is handled by a purpose-built model, not one general-purpose AI guessing at everything. They share results and reason about them together.

01

Scan your skin

Upload a face photo. A vision model estimates tone, type, acne, and dark-circle likelihood — each with a real statistical confidence range.

02

Scan a product

Photograph an ingredients label. OCR reads it, then we cross-check every ingredient against a 28,000-name reference database.

03

Get matched

A content-based recommender suggests products for your profile — every suggestion comes with a plain-language reason, never a black box.

04

Check a concern

Describe a symptom in text — no photo needed, ever, so private areas can be discussed comfortably. Cross-referenced with weather and product history.

Why trust it

We show our uncertainty, on purpose

Most AI tools give you one confident-sounding number. SkinIQ tells you when it's actually sure — and when it isn't.

  • Every prediction carries a statistically calibrated confidence range, not a made-up percentage.
  • When the model is unsure, you can self-report instead of trusting a shaky guess.
  • Every agent decision is logged to an auditable trail — nothing is a black box.
  • We never diagnose. Every result nudges toward a licensed dermatologist for anything that matters.
